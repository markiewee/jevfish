# Example brief: weekly cleaning upgrade at Lazybee

This is an example seed document for JevFish. Prices and survey numbers below are illustrative, not real Lazybee data.

## The company

Lazybee is a small Singapore co-living operator run by Makery Pte Ltd. It rents furnished private rooms in shared apartments at Thomson Grove, Ivory Heights and Chiltern Park. Rooms come with Wi-Fi, air-conditioning, a shared kitchen, and a washer and dryer. Cats are allowed. Viewings run on Saturday mornings and bookings go through book.lazybee.sg. Mark Wee runs the business with Jason Park.

## The proposal

Lazybee is considering a cleaning upgrade. Today common areas are cleaned every two weeks. The proposal is weekly professional cleaning of common areas plus one room clean a month, with rent going up by S$100 a month (example figure) for new tenants.

## What renters say

In an informal chat with current tenants, several said messy housemates are the main reason they would move out. Others said the rent is already at the top of what they can pay, and that they would rather split cleaning duties themselves. Foreign professionals on Employment Passes tend to value convenience and are less price sensitive. International students compare rooms mostly on price. Pet owners say cat-friendly rooms are hard to find in Singapore.

## The market

Singapore renters compare co-living rooms with HDB and condo room rentals on price, commute, cleanliness and privacy. A larger co-living chain, Coliving Co (a fictional competitor for this example), recently advertised professional cleaning as standard. Property agents on listing sites often push cheaper HDB rooms to students.

## The decision

Mark wants to know whether the cleaning upgrade at a higher rent would make more renters book a viewing, compared with keeping the current offer.
